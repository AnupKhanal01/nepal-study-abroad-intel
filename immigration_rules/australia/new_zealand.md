# New Zealand — student visa/immigration checklist

> **last_verified:** 2026-09-13 (general knowledge current to Jan 2026 — re-verify TO VERIFY
> items against Immigration New Zealand; work-rights and post-study-work rules changed
> materially in 2024 and are worth a fresh check every cycle.)

- **Region:** Australia/Oceania
- **Visa/permit name:** **Student visa** (a "Pathway Student Visa" option exists for a sequence
  of up to 3 consecutive courses)
- **Issuing authority:** Immigration New Zealand (INZ), part of the Ministry of Business,
  Innovation and Employment (MBIE)

## Entry / academic requirements
- Institution-set; INZ requires the course/provider to be accredited and the institution to
  issue an Offer of Place.

## English language requirement
- Institution-set (commonly IELTS ~6.0+ for degree-level) — TO VERIFY per program.

## Financial / bank requirement
- Must show funds for tuition plus a living-cost amount set by INZ per month/year (revised
  periodically) — exact current figure: TO VERIFY.

## Health insurance
- Mandatory approved medical/travel insurance for the visa duration — cost: TO VERIFY.

## Visa/permit process
- Online application via INZ; supporting documents include the Offer of Place, funds evidence,
  and (per course) any specific requirements from the institution.
- Processing time and fee: TO VERIFY.

## Work rights while studying
- Work-rights entitlement now depends on qualification level — INZ tightened rules in 2024 so
  that lower-level (non-degree) qualifications may have **no or reduced** work rights, while
  degree-level study typically retains up to ~20 hrs/week during term, full-time during breaks —
  TO VERIFY current rule for the specific qualification level being marketed, since agencies may
  not flag this distinction clearly.

## Post-study work / stay-back route
- **Post-Study Work Visa** eligibility was significantly tightened in 2024 — it is now generally
  tied to studying a qualification on an approved list / at a certain level, rather than being
  broadly available as before. TO VERIFY current eligibility list before treating any agency's
  "guaranteed work visa after graduation" claim as accurate — this is one of the most
  commonly oversold points in recruitment marketing for NZ.

## Dependents
- Rules depend on qualification level and visa type; historically more restricted than before
  the 2024 changes — TO VERIFY current policy.

## Cost of living reference
- INZ publishes its own living-cost figure for visa purposes — prefer that over an agency
  estimate once verified.

## Notes specific to Jan/Apr 2027 intake
- New Zealand academic years typically start in **late Feb/March**, with a mid-year intake
  around **July** for some programs — confirm what "Jan 2027"/"Apr 2027" actually refers to
  (often a pathway/foundation start rather than the main degree intake).
- Given the 2024 tightening of work rights and post-study work eligibility, be especially
  skeptical of agency claims implying easy work/stay-back outcomes; verify against the current
  approved-qualification list.

## Sources to check when verifying
- Immigration New Zealand (immigration.govt.nz).
