# Ireland — student visa/immigration checklist

> **last_verified:** 2026-09-13 (general knowledge current to Jan 2026 — re-verify all TO VERIFY
> items against the Irish Immigration Service before advising anyone.)

- **Region:** Europe
- **Visa/permit name:** "D" study visa on entry, then registration for a **Stamp 2** (or Stamp 2A
  for non-degree programs) immigration permission
- **Issuing authority:** Irish Immigration Service Delivery (ISD), Department of Justice
- **Relevant for Nepal applicants because:** Nepal is a visa-required country for Ireland;
  applications go through the Irish embassy/visa office covering Nepal — confirm current
  jurisdiction (historically New Delhi).

## Entry / academic requirements
- Set by the institution; no separate government academic bar beyond a genuine, valid offer.

## English language requirement
- Institution-set (commonly IELTS ~6.0–6.5); immigration approval also requires the course to be
  on the interim list of eligible programs (ILEP) for non-EEA students at private colleges.

## Financial / bank requirement
- Must show funds to cover fees and living costs; historically referenced amount ~€7,000+/year
  living costs plus fees — TO VERIFY current figure.
- Non-EEA students at private colleges must show the college holds **Learner Protection** (fee
  protection insurance) — check this before recommending a private college.

## Health insurance
- Mandatory private medical insurance for the duration of stay — TO VERIFY typical cost.

## Visa/permit process
- "D" visa application (if visa-required) + registration with immigration (previously Garda
  National Immigration Bureau, now ISD) after arrival for the residence permission card.
- Processing time and fee: TO VERIFY.

## Work rights while studying
- Typically up to 20 hours/week during term, up to 40 hours/week during official college
  holiday periods — confirm current cap and which course levels qualify.

## Post-study work / stay-back route
- **Third Level Graduate Programme** (Stamp 1G): historically 1 year for Level 8 (bachelor's)
  graduates, up to 2 years for Level 9+ (master's/PhD) — TO VERIFY current duration.

## Dependents
- Generally not a standard feature of the student route for taught programs — TO VERIFY for
  research/PhD candidates.

## Cost of living reference
- Dublin is materially more expensive than other Irish cities — note the specific campus city
  when comparing cost-of-living claims from agency posts.

## Notes specific to Jan/Apr 2027 intake
- Ireland's non-EEA student intake and course-eligibility list (ILEP) can change; confirm the
  specific program is still ILEP-listed close to application time.

## Sources to check when verifying
- Irish Immigration Service Delivery, domain: irishimmigration.ie
