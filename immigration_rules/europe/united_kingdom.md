# United Kingdom — student visa/immigration checklist

> **last_verified:** 2026-09-13 (based on general knowledge current to Jan 2026 — this file
> mixes stable structural facts with figures/caps that change often. Re-verify every TO VERIFY
> item on GOV.UK before advising anyone, especially for a 2027 intake.)

- **Region:** Europe
- **Visa/permit name:** Student visa (route previously called Tier 4 (General))
- **Issuing authority:** UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI), part of the Home Office
- **Relevant for Nepal applicants because:** Visa applications for Nepal-based applicants are
  processed via a UK visa application centre in Nepal (VFS Global-operated); Nepal has
  historically been flagged in UK Home Office "credibility interview" sampling for student
  visas — TO VERIFY current risk-profiling status.

## Entry / academic requirements
- University sets its own academic entry bar; UKVI itself doesn't set an academic minimum, but
  requires a valid, unconditional offer confirmed via CAS.
- Recognized equivalency: universities assess Nepali SLC/SEE, +2, and Bachelor's transcripts
  case-by-case — no blanket UK government equivalency table.

## English language requirement
- Most institutions require IELTS (Academic) or an approved SELT; typical bachelor's ~6.0
  overall, master's ~6.5 overall (no band below a set minimum) — TO VERIFY per university.
- UKVI itself requires evidence of English ability at CEFR B2+ for the Student route unless the
  applicant is exempt (e.g. prior study in English, majority-English-speaking country).

## Financial / bank requirement
- Maintenance funds: must show funds covering tuition (or remaining balance) plus a fixed
  monthly living-cost figure for up to 9 months (London vs outside-London rates differ).
- Funds must typically be held for a **28-consecutive-day period** ending no more than ~31 days
  before the application.
- Exact monthly living-cost figures: TO VERIFY on GOV.UK ("Student visa: money") — these are
  revised periodically.

## Health insurance
- **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)** is mandatory, paid as part of the visa application,
  calculated per year of visa length. Amount: TO VERIFY (revised periodically by the Home
  Office).

## Visa/permit process
- Application lodged online, biometrics + document submission at a VAC (VFS Global) in Nepal.
- Requires a CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) number from the university, issued
  after deposit/conditions are met.
- Certain subjects (mostly STEM/dual-use research areas) require **ATAS** (Academic Technology
  Approval Scheme) clearance for postgraduate study — check per course.
- Typical processing time and visa fee: TO VERIFY on GOV.UK.

## Work rights while studying
- Degree-level student visa holders: typically up to 20 hours/week during term, full-time during
  scheduled vacations — confirm current cap and any course-level exceptions.

## Post-study work / stay-back route
- **Graduate Route**: post-study work visa, historically 2 years after a bachelor's/master's, 3
  years after a PhD — TO VERIFY current duration and any eligibility changes.

## Dependents
- Since a 2024 policy tightening, most taught postgraduate (master's) students on the Student
  route **cannot** bring dependents. Dependents are generally still permitted for PhD/research
  programs and government-sponsored scholarship students — TO VERIFY current rule, this is a
  frequently-cited change that affects student decision-making and is worth re-checking for
  every intake cycle.

## Cost of living reference
- UKVI publishes its own maintenance living-cost figures (separate from general cost-of-living
  estimates) — use the GOV.UK figure for visa purposes, not an agency's estimate.

## Notes specific to Jan/Apr 2027 intake
- UK student visa policy (dependents, Graduate Route duration, salary/skill thresholds
  post-study) has changed multiple times in recent years — treat any agency claim about "current
  rules" as provisional until checked against GOV.UK at time of application.

## Sources to check when verifying
- GOV.UK — Student visa pages (Home Office / UKVI), domain: gov.uk
