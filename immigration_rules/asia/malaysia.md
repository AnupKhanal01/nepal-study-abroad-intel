# Malaysia — student visa/immigration checklist

> **last_verified:** 2026-09-13 (general knowledge current to Jan 2026 — re-verify TO VERIFY
> items against EMGS/Immigration Department of Malaysia.)

- **Region:** Asia
- **Visa/permit name:** **Student Pass** (with accompanying Single Entry Visa where applicable)
- **Issuing authority:** Immigration Department of Malaysia, with applications processed through
  **Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS)** on behalf of the Ministry of Higher Education.
- **Relevant for Nepal applicants because:** Malaysia is a popular lower-cost destination
  marketed heavily to Nepali students; EMGS centralizes and standardizes the visa process
  regardless of institution, which can be a useful cross-check against agency claims.

## Entry / academic requirements
- Institution-set; EMGS itself mainly validates that the institution and program are properly
  registered/accredited (check MQA — Malaysian Qualifications Agency — accreditation).

## English language requirement
- Institution-set (commonly IELTS ~5.5–6.5 depending on level) — TO VERIFY per program; some
  institutions offer English foundation/bridging pathways for lower scores.

## Financial / bank requirement
- EMGS/immigration require proof of sufficient funds for tuition and living costs; specific
  amount: TO VERIFY.

## Health insurance
- Mandatory health insurance/coverage scheme via EMGS for the duration of study — cost:
  TO VERIFY (EMGS typically arranges or mandates a specific scheme).

## Visa/permit process
- Institution submits the Student Pass application to EMGS; EMGS issues a Visa Approval Letter
  (VAL) used to obtain a Single Entry Visa (if required) to enter Malaysia, after which the
  Student Pass is endorsed on arrival.
- Processing time and fee: TO VERIFY (EMGS publishes indicative processing timelines).

## Work rights while studying
- Historically quite restricted: limited part-time work (where permitted at all) generally
  capped around 20 hours/week and only during semester breaks, subject to approval — TO VERIFY
  current policy, as Malaysia's stance on student part-time work has shifted periodically.

## Post-study work / stay-back route
- Limited compared to UK/Australia/Canada; some pass-conversion or employment-pass routes exist
  for graduates who secure a job — TO VERIFY current mechanism.

## Dependents
- Generally not a standard feature of the Student Pass route — TO VERIFY for exceptions.

## Cost of living reference
- Kuala Lumpur vs other cities (Penang, Johor, etc.) differ meaningfully — capture the specific
  city; Malaysia is generally marketed as low-cost, which agencies should be asked to itemize
  rather than take at face value.

## Notes specific to Jan/Apr 2027 intake
- Malaysian private institutions often run multiple intakes per year (commonly Jan/Feb, May,
  Sep) — confirm the specific institution's intake calendar rather than assuming a fixed
  Jan/Apr cycle.

## Sources to check when verifying
- Education Malaysia Global Services (educationmalaysia.gov.my / emgs.com.my), Immigration
  Department of Malaysia.
