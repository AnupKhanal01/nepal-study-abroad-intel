## Caution: verify before trusting a page

Impersonator/scam pages copying real consultancy branding are common in this market. Before
logging a post as coming from one of these agencies, sanity-check:
- The page has a verified badge or a long posting history consistent with a real business.
- Contact details/links match the agency's known website, not a lookalike domain.
- The post isn't asking for payment through an unofficial channel (a common scam pattern).
